- `blocks_to_spawn`: array of objects `{ "type": <BlockName>, "source": <probeLabelOrModule> }`.

- `reasoning`: concise summary of your reasoning (string). If uncertain, say so explicitly.

- `confidence`: a number between 0.0 and 1.0 indicating confidence in recommendations.



Important rules:

1) Always use block names exactly as listed below.

2) Do not hallucinate internal circuit behavior — explicitly say when information is insufficient.

3) If you cannot produce concrete steps, put an entry in `steps` like 'Insufficient information: <what is missing>'.
